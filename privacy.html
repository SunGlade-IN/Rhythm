Important Medical, Contraception & Emergency Notice

Not a Medical Device or Contraceptive Method: Rhythm Cycle Tracker ("Rhythm") is designed solely for personal wellness awareness and informational cycle logging. It is not a certified medical device, clinical diagnostic tool, or healthcare service.

Strictly Not for Birth Control or Family Planning: Do not rely on Rhythm's cycle estimates, ovulation projections, fertile window calculations, or Partner Companion Mode alerts/notifications to prevent or achieve pregnancy, or for natural family planning. Biological cycles fluctuate naturally due to stress, travel, illness, hormonal shifts, and conditions such as PCOS. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, contraception, or treatment.

Medical Emergencies: If you experience severe pelvic or abdominal pain, abnormal heavy bleeding, dizziness, fainting, or any acute medical symptoms, call your local emergency services (such as 112 or 911) or seek immediate emergency medical care.

Part I

Privacy Policy

01 Developer Identity & Overview

Rhythm Cycle Tracker (Android package ID: in.tracker.cycle.rhythm) is developed and published by SunGlade, an independent developer based in India ("we", "us", or "our"). For any privacy questions, feedback, or data inquiries, you can reach us directly at admin.rhythm@gmail.com.

Rhythm is built with an offline-first architecture. We do not operate backend servers, user accounts, or remote databases. The application explicitly removes the Android android.permission.INTERNET permission from its manifest, meaning the app itself does not transmit your logged health records to us or any external server.

02 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All cycle and wellness information you enter into Rhythm is stored exclusively on your physical device:

  • Cycle & Wellness Logs (Encrypted Database): Period start and end dates, period irregularity flags, flow intensity, flow texture (such as fluid, spotting, or clots), menstrual product used, pain levels, physical symptoms, mood, energy levels, sleep quality, digestion, skin observations, physical activity levels, medication notes, medication-effectiveness checkboxes, hydration counts, cervical mucus observations, and personal daily notes are stored in a local Room SQLite database encrypted at rest using SQLCipher (256-bit AES) with keys managed by the Android Hardware Keystore.
  • Profile, Settings, Partner-Sync Cache, Custom Avatar & Widget Summary Storage (Private Sandbox): Your age is requested during initial setup to apply Rhythm's age limits. Optional profile details (such as display name, gender/sex, height, weight, wellness goals, and cycle tracking mode), app settings (such as PIN configuration and reminder preferences), current cycle status values displayed on home-screen widgets (current cycle day, phase name, days until next period, and companion icon), and imported Partner Companion Mode predictions, cadence metrics, and care notes are kept in the application's private, OS-sandboxed Android Jetpack DataStore storage, which is separate from the SQLCipher-encrypted database. If you select a custom profile picture, the selected image is copied into the app's private internal file directory (filesDir).
  • OS Cloud Backups Disabled: Automatic operating-system cloud backups (Google Drive device backup) and ADB device-to-device transfers are explicitly disabled (allowBackup="false") for Rhythm's database and preference directories so your logs are not uploaded to cloud storage without your knowledge.

03 Important Warning Regarding Data Loss & Device Changes

Because your database encryption key is hardware-bound to your specific device and cloud backups are disabled, we have no way to access, view, or recover your data:

  • Device Loss, Reset, or Uninstall: If you lose or damage your phone, switch to a new phone, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Rhythm, all locally stored cycle history and logs will be permanently lost unless you have previously exported a manual backup.
  • Forgotten PIN: Because there are no user accounts or servers, there is no remote "Forgot PIN" recovery option. If you forget your 4-digit PIN and do not have Biometric Lock enabled, you will need to clear the application's storage in Android settings to use the app again, which permanently erases all stored data.
  • How to Move Your Data: To preserve your records when upgrading phones, use the in-app manual JSON Backup Export feature prior to switching devices.

05 Shared Devices, Home-Screen Widgets, Notifications & Screen Protection

If other people have physical access to your or your partner's phone, cycle information could be viewed unless appropriate privacy and OS settings are configured:

  • App Lock (PIN & Biometrics): Enable App Lock in Settings to require authentication whenever Rhythm is opened or resumed from the background.
  • Discreet Mode: Replaces explicit menstrual and fertility terminology on in-app dashboard cards and notifications with neutral wellness phrasing.
  • Screen Capture Block: When App Lock or Discreet Mode is active, Rhythm enables Android's FLAG_SECURE window flag inside the app to block screenshots, screen recording, and previews in the recent-apps switcher.
  • Home-Screen Widgets & Partner Compass Visibility: Rhythm includes optional Android home-screen widgets (Rhythm, Harmony, and Companion widgets) as well as Partner Companion views/compasses for quick at-a-glance access to cycle day, phase, days until next period, daily companion tips, and care guidance. Home-screen widgets sit outside the application's PIN/Biometric App Lock, Discreet Mode masking, and FLAG_SECURE screen protection, meaning anyone who can view an unlocked home screen can see them. If you or a partner share a device or want maximum visual discretion, do not place Rhythm widgets on the home screen (or remove them at any time by long-pressing the widget).
  • Partner Notification Channels & Lock-Screen Visibility: Partner Companion Mode uses dedicated notification alerts—such as Day 1 Care Alerts, High-Energy & Ovulation Alerts, and Morning Wingman Briefings. While Discreet Mode phrases notifications neutrally when enabled, notification banners can still appear on an unlocked screen or lock screen unless Android lock-screen privacy settings are configured to hide sensitive notification content.

06 User-Initiated Exports (Unencrypted File Warning)

Rhythm allows you to export your records as PDF summaries, CSV tables, or JSON backup files using Android's system file picker (Storage Access Framework) or system share sheet.

Security Warning for Exported Files: Once you export a PDF, CSV, or JSON file outside Rhythm, that exported file is stored in plain, unencrypted format so that it can be read by PDF viewers, spreadsheet apps, or restored on a new phone. Anyone with access to your device's file folders, email, or cloud drive where you choose to save or share that file will be able to read its contents. You are solely responsible for safeguarding or deleting exported files after use.

07 Partner Companion Mode & Peer-to-Peer Sync

Rhythm offers an optional Partner Companion Mode so a primary user can share high-level cycle cadence and care guidance with a chosen partner without using cloud servers:

  • No Rhythm Servers, No Cloud Accounts: Partner Mode does not use backend servers, user accounts, or cloud databases. Data synchronization occurs strictly peer-to-peer and offline via user-generated QR codes, direct file transfers (such as JSON sync packages), or plain-text summaries initiated manually by the primary user.
  • Strict Data Minimization ("Privacy Shield"):
    • What IS Transferred: Display name, current bleeding status (isBleeding), export date, days-until-next-period countdown, cycle cadence metrics (average cycle length, period duration), start and end dates of recent periods, current cycle day, estimated phase/season, calculated prediction anchors (estimated next period, estimated fertile/ovulation window, cadence variance), general aggregate energy scores, cycle tracking mode, and optional user-entered care or comfort notes. The optional plain-text summary also includes energy and "social battery" descriptions along with phase advice. Care notes are included in what you share, so avoid putting sensitive details in them.
    • What Is NEVER Transferred: Daily symptom logs (such as cramps, headache, or nausea), flow intensity, flow texture, cervical mucus observations, pain scale ratings, medication logs, personal diary notes, and hydration counters. Intimate wellness details remain exclusively on the primary user's phone.
  • Unencrypted Sync Format & Transit Security Warning: Sync codes, JSON sync packages, QR code images, and text summaries are plain, readable text and are not encrypted—meaning anyone who obtains a QR image, file, or text summary can read all of the transferred information. When a user chooses to share a QR image, sync file, or plain-text summary through a third-party messaging app, SMS, email, or cloud service (such as WhatsApp, Telegram, or email), that shared artifact leaves Rhythm's local sandbox and becomes subject to the security, encryption, retention, and privacy practices of the chosen communication service.
  • Partner Device Storage & Isolation: When a partner imports a sync package, the period dates are saved in Rhythm's encrypted database and replace any period history already on that device. The sender's display name replaces the name set on that device. Predictions, cadence metrics, and care notes are kept in the app's private DataStore, which is not covered by the SQLCipher encryption. Rhythm shows a warning when you switch to Partner Mode on a phone that has your own logs. Export a backup first, or use Partner Mode on a phone where you don't track your own cycle.

09 Data Retention, Security Summary & Deletion

Security Summary: Your health logs are protected on-device via a 256-bit AES SQLCipher encrypted database, an Android Hardware Keystore-managed encryption key, optional PIN/biometric App Lock, peer-to-peer data minimization in Partner Mode, and zero internet permission requested by the app.

  • Local Retention Only: Your data stays exclusively on your device until you choose to delete it. Because Rhythm has no backend servers or user accounts, we hold zero copies of your records.
  • Permanent Deletion on Primary Device ("Wipe All App Data & Settings"): Tapping Settings → Wipe All App Data & Settings inside the app, clearing the app's storage in Android system settings, or uninstalling Rhythm permanently and irreversibly deletes all stored logs, history, and preferences from your device. It also removes your custom profile picture, any export files Rhythm saved in its own app folder, and your reminders. It can't delete files you exported or shared elsewhere.
  • Partner Device Deletion ("Disconnect / Clear Synced Rhythm"): On a partner device, Settings → Partner Mode → Disconnect / Clear Synced Rhythm deletes all period history and daily logs stored on that device (including any you entered yourself), the synced predictions, cadence metrics, care notes, and imported display name, cancels partner notifications, and refreshes home-screen widgets. Your theme, PIN, and other app settings are kept.
  • Independent Life Cycles: Because Rhythm has no cloud server or live two-way sync link, deleting or resetting data on one phone does not automatically delete data already imported onto another phone.
  • Exported & Shared Files: Any PDF, CSV, JSON sync files, QR images, or text messages exported or shared outside Rhythm's sandbox must be managed or deleted manually by you.

10 Your Rights & Data Control

Because all data resides locally on your device, you can exercise your data rights (including access, correction, portability, revocation/withdrawal of sharing, and erasure) directly inside the application at any time without needing to submit a request to us:

  • Access & Correction: View or edit any logged period date, symptom, note, or profile field directly within the Calendar and Settings screens.
  • Portability: Export your complete data in JSON, CSV, or PDF format via Settings.
  • Revoking Partner Sharing: Because Partner Companion Mode shares offline point-in-time snapshots rather than a live cloud stream, the primary user cannot remotely recall or erase data already imported onto a partner's device. To stop sharing future updates, stop generating or sending new sync codes/exports; to remove previously synced data, the partner must tap Settings → Partner Mode → Disconnect / Clear Synced Rhythm (or Wipe All App Data & Settings / uninstall) on their own device.
  • Questions or Grievances: Contact the developer at admin.rhythm@gmail.com.

11 Age Requirement (13+ / 18+ for Partner Mode) & Minor Privacy

Rhythm is intended for users aged 13 and older. Rhythm asks for your age during initial setup; if you enter an age under 13, the app does not let you continue. Rhythm relies on the age you enter and does not independently verify it. You can update your age later in Settings, except while Partner Companion Mode is active. Partner Companion Mode is available only when the age entered is 18 or older.

  • Minors (Ages 13 to 17): If you enter an age under 18, all Partner Companion Mode features are hidden, including generating or scanning sync QR codes. You can still back up your data and export CSV or PDF summaries—for example, to share with a doctor. Please review this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, and the Medical Disclaimer with a parent or legal guardian before using the app. Because age is self-declared, parents and guardians remain responsible for how Rhythm is used by a minor in their care. Optional tips via Google Play Billing require the permission of a parent or the legal account holder.
  • No Server Collection or Profiling of Minors: Because Rhythm stores all logs locally on the user's device and includes no analytics, advertising, or network transmission capabilities, we do not collect, store on external servers, or behaviorally profile personal data from users of any age.
